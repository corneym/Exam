Curriculum management

Curriculum structure

Exam Question Bank organises curriculum information by Subject and syllabus version, with curriculum nodes such as Unit, Topic, Subtopic and Descriptor.

Import curriculum

Choose Curriculum → Import... to import curriculum information through the supported curriculum import workflow.

Author or edit curriculum

Choose Curriculum → Author / Edit... to create or maintain curriculum structure.

Changes made here affect the curriculum data available for classification, searching, mapping and Revision Output.

Review mappings

Choose Curriculum → Review Mappings... to review mappings between historical curriculum classifications and the current syllabus.

Confirmed mappings allow historically classified Questions to become applicable to current curriculum locations without replacing their original stored classification.

One historical classification may legitimately map to more than one current Descriptor.
